# TaskFlow Knowledge Base

## Plans

TaskFlow has Starter, Team, and Business plans.

The Starter plan supports up to 3 users.

The Team plan supports up to 25 users.

The Business plan supports unlimited users and includes priority support.

## Cancellation

Customers can cancel at any time from the Billing page.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

TaskFlow does not provide automatic refunds for unused subscription time.

## Support

Starter customers receive email support within two business days.

Team customers receive support within one business day.

Business customers receive priority support.

## Data Export

Workspace administrators can export workspace data as CSV from Settings > Data Export.

Exports may take up to 24 hours for large workspaces.

## Security

TaskFlow does not provide customer passwords or private workspace data to other users.

Support staff may request verification before discussing account-specific information.
